# Space Asset Credits & Attribution

The visual design system of AstroHealth uses a layered deep-space atmosphere:
- **Layer 1 & 2**: Programmatic CSS/Canvas starfield and radial gradients simulating deep space.
- **Layer 3**: Programmatic CSS and SVG-rendered Earth limb curvature and atmospheric glow fallback at the top-right and sidebar bottom, inspired by orbital imagery of Earth's horizon against deep space.
- No third-party proprietary assets, copyrighted astronaut illustrations, or official NASA agency logos are used, maintaining full compliance and avoiding any implied agency endorsement.
